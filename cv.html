Profile

Building upon a solid foundation in civil and environmental engineering, I have specialised in optical sensing and geomatics. Over the past decade, my research has focused on advancing hyperspectral imaging methods to map biological, physical, and chemical attributes of aquatic and terrestrial biophysical systems.

My expertise spans the entire research spectrum — from technical payload design, data acquisition, and processing to academic dissemination. I am highly proficient in multidimensional data analysis, image processing, and the development of bio-optical models to retrieve biochemical features through statistical learning and radiative transfer modelling.

I have participated in and led fieldwork campaigns in the Arctic (6×), Antarctic (4×), and other remote locations. I have resided in Italy, Mexico, Chile, Argentina, Denmark, Australia, and currently Japan.
